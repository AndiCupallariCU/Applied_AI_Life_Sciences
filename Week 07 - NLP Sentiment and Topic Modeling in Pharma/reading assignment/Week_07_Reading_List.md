# Week 7 — Reading List

NLP in Pharma Commercial: adverse events, field notes and social listening. Sources are limited to regulations and agency guidance, peer-reviewed papers, standard texts, and the IQVIA papers in the course library (copied into this folder). Open-access copies: run `bash download_sources.sh` (needs internet) — they land in `sources/`.
